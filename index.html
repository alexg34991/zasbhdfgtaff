Frequently asked questions
How long does a claim take?

Review time depends on how busy staff are. You will get a Discord DM as soon as your ticket has a result, so there is no need to keep checking.

What do I need to submit a claim?

Your VRFS ID and a link to the message that proves your claim. Pick the item from Our Items and fill both in on the ticket form.

My ticket was denied. What now?

Check the reason in your DM, fix whatever was missing and submit a new ticket. If you think it was a mistake, use Talk to Staff.

I am not receiving DMs.

Make sure your Discord privacy settings allow direct messages from server members, then check My Claim Tickets for the status.
